Quarterly Protocol & Kill Criteria

The 90-day ritual (under 15 min)

  1. Pull the 1-month median PE (from the source's 1M PE chart) and the 5-Yr median PE for Nifty 50 / 100 / Midcap 150 / Smallcap 250 — one source, consolidated, same PE type. Read on a fixed quarter-end date.
  2. For any cheap segment, glance at whether earnings are collapsing (value trap). The one check the tool can't make.
  3. Enter the numbers. Read the Explorer column, add fixed Core = Total to set on Coin.
  4. Edit SIPs → close → forget 90 days.

The engine (continuous — v6.1)

Score = base × (5-Yr median ÷ 1-month median PE), normalized over tilted segments. Cheaper → smoothly higher weight; richer → smoothly lower. No 0.95/1.05 bands, no discrete steps, no cliffs — a 0.6% PE wobble moves the split by rupees, not thousands. The tilt strength k is locked at 1.0 (weight scales 1:1 with cheapness) — a deliberate design constant, not a live knob. When all segments are equally (over/under)valued, it deploys at base weights. No cash parking — market-top protection lives in your ₹52k opportunity fund.

Pre-committed kill criteria

  • K1 — Whipsaw: now near-impossible (continuous). If the split still lurches on tiny PE moves → investigate the data feed.
  • K3 — Friction: quarterly run >15 min or makes you hesitate → kill.
  • K4 — No edge: judged at 8 quarters (2 yrs). If explorer doesn't beat fixed 18/32/36/14 + rebalance by ≥0.25%/yr → kill. One quarter is pure noise.
